HERE TO HELP

Frequently asked questions

Can I pay with M-Pesa?

Checkout records a local demo order with payment pending. It does not send an M-Pesa request or take payment.

Does my shopping bag stay saved?

Your bag and wishlist are saved in this browser on this device. Clearing this site's browser data removes them.

How do I find a product?

Search by product name, category, or brand, or use the category filters. Search results update as you type.

Are orders shipped or fulfilled?

No. Orders are saved only in this browser for demonstration. No payment, shipment, or fulfillment takes place.

DELIVERY INFORMATION

Shipping & returns

This site saves demo orders in your browser. It does not calculate delivery charges, accept payments, ship products, or process returns.

Before enabling real sales, TECHZONE must publish delivery areas, delivery estimates, applicable charges, return eligibility, and a working support contact.

YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy policy

This demo does not have a server-side account, order, or mailing-list service. Product search runs in your browser. Your shopping bag, wishlist, order history, and checkout details are stored in this browser's local storage.

Order records include the name, phone, email, delivery address, products, and order status you provide. They are stored locally on this device and are not sent to TECHZONE. Do not enter sensitive personal information in this demo. To remove locally saved data, clear this site's browser storage.

If you submit the newsletter form, your email address is saved in this browser only. It is not sent to TECHZONE or an email provider, and no newsletter will be sent.

The page loads fonts from Google Fonts and product imagery from Unsplash; those providers may receive the usual connection information when your browser requests those assets.

PLEASE NOTE

Terms of use

TECHZONE is presented as a non-transactional storefront demo. Product listings, prices, ratings, shipping benefits, and checkout interactions are for demonstration and do not constitute an offer to sell. Checkout creates only a local demo record; it does not accept payment or create a real sale.

INCLUSIVE BY DESIGN

Accessibility

The storefront includes semantic headings and controls, labels for form fields, keyboard-operable buttons, and reduced-motion support. If you encounter an accessibility barrier, this demo currently has no support channel; a contact method should be added before launch.
